# PLAYERTRACKER™ statuis-free                   Creation date- 8/1/2026                              Completion date- NA
A minecraft mod that auto finds players bases for you! Tired of constantly being on a minecraft server when you cant find anyones base! I present to you the player tracker. No matter how far away a player is, this mod will track the closest player.


Tired of exstream work to find someones base in a server or ever your friends base in yalls minecraft world? Well there is now a solution! The new PLAYERTRACKER™ mod allows you to walk directly to where your friends base is! While in servers, people love to afk in their base. With that said when you toggle this tool, you will directly walk tords the nearest player (Weather that is 1000 blocks away or 25000 blocks away.)
